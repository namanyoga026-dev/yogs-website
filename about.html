About YogaLife

Making authentic yoga accessible to every household in India — for free, forever.

What Drives Us

Mission & Vision

🎯

Our Mission

To bring the ancient science of yoga to every doorstep in India, breaking barriers of cost, location, and language. We believe wellness is not a luxury — it's a right.

🔭

Our Vision

A healthier India where every citizen has access to world-class yoga training, creating a ripple effect of physical fitness, mental clarity, and spiritual growth across generations.

Milestones

Our Journey

2012

The Beginning

Started with a single yoga class under a banyan tree with just 15 students in Rishikesh.

2015

Going Digital

Launched our first online classes, reaching 10,000+ students across 50 cities in India.

2018

1 Lakh Users

Crossed 1 lakh active daily practitioners. Featured in national media for the first time.

2020

Pandemic Response

Made all classes free during COVID-19. User base grew 10x in 6 months to 50 lakh+ members.

2024

1.4 Crore+ Community

Now India's largest free yoga platform with 450+ certified instructors across all 28 states.
